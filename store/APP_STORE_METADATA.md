## App Name
The Vault — Luxury Handbag Rentals

## Subtitle (iOS) / Short Description (Android)
Designer Bags, On Demand.

## Long Description
The Vault is a mobile way to rent designer handbags for the days you want one — an event, a trip,
or an ordinary Tuesday that deserves better.

Browse a curated collection sorted into Iconic, Premium and Contemporary tiers. Open any bag for
its photos, details, daily pricing and an availability calendar, then pick the dates you want and
take it through checkout in a few taps.

Why The Vault?
- CURATED COLLECTION: Browse bags by tier, with photos, pricing and full descriptions on every listing.
- AVAILABILITY AT A GLANCE: A calendar on each bag shows the dates that are open before you commit.
- SIMPLE BOOKING: Choose your dates, review the total, and confirm in one flow.
- YOUR ACCOUNT: Create an account, sign in securely, and keep every booking in one place under Orders.
- BUILT FOR BROWSING: A quiet, editorial interface designed to stay out of the way of the bags.

How it Works:
1. BROWSE: Explore the collection by tier — Iconic, Premium, Contemporary.
2. CHOOSE: Open a bag for photos, details, pricing and open dates on the calendar.
3. BOOK: Select your rental dates and confirm your booking.
4. KEEP TRACK: Your bookings live in the Orders tab of your account.

Download The Vault and find your next bag.
